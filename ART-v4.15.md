# v4.15 일러스트 제작 기록

제작 방식: Codex 내장 image_gen. 원본 PNG의 투명도를 보존하고 게임에서 표시 영역만 지정했다. 외부 유료 API나 힉스필드 크레딧은 사용하지 않았다.

몬스터 6종 × 4자세, 지역 아이템 12종, 배경 장식 4종을 8개 아틀라스로 제작했다. 조수종 소라게는 v4.14 원본을 함께 사용한다.

## shoreSnail

저장: [assets/shoreSnailV415.png](assets/shoreSnailV415.png)

최종 프롬프트:

Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent 2D sprite sheet for cozy Korean fantasy RPG Our Dreamy Nights. ONE square image, exactly 2 by 2 equal cells, four poses of the SAME creature: a small pearl-shelled sea snail with coral horns, sandy cream body and navy scarf; a shy beach creature guarding washed-up keepsakes. All face RIGHT, full body side view. Top left idle, top right withdraw head and lower shell, bottom left slide forward with head lowered, bottom right recoiling with eyes shut after a hit. Soft hand-painted anime illustration, warm brown clean outlines, pearl highlights, muted teal/coral/gold palette. Match cute chibi heroes in a dreamy harbor. Each creature entirely within its own cell with at least 12 percent transparent padding on ALL sides, feet aligned to 85 percent of cell height, equal creature scale in all cells. Truly transparent alpha background. No ground, scenery, text, grid, border, UI, checkerboard, watermark. Clear readable silhouettes, not frightening.
